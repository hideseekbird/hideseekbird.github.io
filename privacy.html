Privacy Policy

Hide & Seek — A British Bird Log
Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Version 1 of Hide & Seek handles information when you use the app.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Hide & Seek is operated by the developer of the app. For privacy questions, contact hide.seek.bird@gmail.com.

2. Information stored by the app

Version 1 may store information you enter or create while using the app, including:

  • your birder name and selected avatar or companion;
  • bird encounters, including Seen or Heard status and bird counts;
  • dates and times associated with your records;
  • locations that you choose to type into the app;
  • notes you choose to enter;
  • Go Birding trip lists and trip titles;
  • habitat placements;
  • SEEDS, rewards, achievements and badges;
  • Big Month and Big Year challenge progress; and
  • app preferences and settings.

3. Where your data is stored

In Version 1, your normal birding records are stored locally on your device. Hide & Seek does not require an account and does not automatically upload your normal birding records to a developer-operated cloud service.

4. Location information

Version 1 does not request or use your device's GPS location. If you type a place name or other location into a birding record or Go Birding trip, that information is stored as part of your local app data.

5. Exporting and importing your data

Hide & Seek includes user-initiated export and import features. If you choose to export your data, the app can create a backup file containing your app information. You decide where that file is saved or shared using your device's system options.

An exported file may contain your profile information, bird logs, manually entered locations, notes, trip records and other saved game data. Once you choose to send or store an exported file using another service, that service's own privacy practices may apply.

6. Information sent to the developer

Version 1 does not include an account system, behavioural analytics, advertising trackers or automatic cloud synchronisation. The app does not automatically send your normal birding log to the developer.

If you contact the developer by email or through the website, the information you choose to include in that message will be received and used to respond to you.

If you join the Hide & Seek mailing list through the website, your email address and related subscription information are collected so that Hide & Seek can send you beta news, product updates and launch information.

7. Third parties and external links

The app or website may contain links to external websites, for example credit or reference pages. Opening an external website creates a normal connection to that website, which may receive information such as your IP address in the ordinary course of providing the page. Hide & Seek does not send your saved birding data to those sites.

The website uses Kit to manage mailing-list sign-ups and email subscriptions. When you submit the mailing-list form, your email address and subscription information are processed by Kit for those purposes. You can review Kit's privacy policy for information about its service.

8. Why information is used

Information stored in Version 1 is used to provide the app's bird-logging, Journal, habitat, challenge, achievement, trip-list and settings features. Information you send directly by email is used to provide support and respond to questions. Mailing-list subscription information is used to send Hide & Seek beta news, product updates and launch information.

9. Retention and deletion

Your local app data remains on your device until it is removed through the app's data controls, the app is uninstalled with its data removed, or the device operating system removes the data. Exported backup files remain wherever you chose to save them until you delete them.

Email messages are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to handle support, enquiries or related record keeping. Mailing-list subscription information is retained while you remain subscribed and as otherwise reasonably necessary for suppression, compliance or record-keeping purposes. You can unsubscribe using the link included in marketing emails.

10. Security

Hide & Seek uses the storage protections provided by the device and operating system. No storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so you should keep control of exported backup files and use appropriate device security.

11. Your UK data protection rights

Where UK data protection law applies to personal information held by the developer, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, depending on the circumstances. Most Version 1 birding data is stored only on your own device and is therefore not held by the developer.

To ask about personal information you have directly provided to the developer, email hide.seek.bird@gmail.com.

12. Children

Version 1 is not designed to create public profiles or collect children's location through GPS. If the developer becomes aware that personal information has been provided directly in a way that requires deletion or another response under applicable law, the developer will take appropriate action.

13. Future online features

Future versions of Hide & Seek may introduce features such as accounts, cloud storage or synchronisation, GPS-based functionality, public usernames, profiles or leaderboards. These features are not part of the Version 1 privacy model described above. This policy will be updated before or when any materially different data practices are introduced.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, website or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact hide.seek.bird@gmail.com.
